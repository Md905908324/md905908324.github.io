Ungate (Work in Progress)

Trading the Surprise (Work in Progress)

An event-driven trading strategy research project that models market reactions to macroeconomic surprises using volatility forecasting, regime detection, and machine learning. The framework is designed to study when surprise-driven trading signals work, how they behave across volatility regimes, and whether volatility-aware position sizing can improve risk-adjusted returns.

Proof Credit

A decentralized undercollateralized lending marketplace built during the Unlink x Monad Hackathon. Designed to challenge DeFi’s overcollateralized model by using private credit verification and zero-knowledge proofs, allowing borrowers to prove income, employment, and creditworthiness without exposing raw data. Contributed across frontend development, wallet and session architecture, frontend-backend integration, dashboard data flows, dynamic risk-scoring logic, and borrower/lender UX.
